Central Water Filtration Installation FAQs

Questions from Central homeowners deciding between whole-house filtration and kitchen reverse osmosis.

Should I install reverse osmosis or a whole house water filter first?

Kitchen-only taste issues often point to RO. If showers and laundry are involved, start with whole house water filtration. We can combine them when both goals matter.

What happens during a typical install?

We shut water, make the connections, restore service, and check fixtures. You leave with a working system and a short explanation of bypass and filter changes.

Do you install water softeners in Central?

Yes, when spotting and soap film point to hardness. Water softener installation is not automatic on every job.
