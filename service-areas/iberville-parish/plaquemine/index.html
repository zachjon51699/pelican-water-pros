Plaquemine Water Filtration FAQs

Installation questions from Plaquemine homeowners.

How do you decide between whole-house and RO?

Whole-house covers every tap. Reverse osmosis is drinking water at the kitchen. We recommend based on which fixtures you want treated.

Can older Plaquemine homes be retrofitted?

Usually yes, if there is a usable shutoff and space for service. We confirm that before installation day.

Do you install water softeners here?

Yes, when mineral scale is the issue. It can be combined with a water filtration system.
