Gonzales Installation FAQs

Common questions from homeowners who want a water filtration system installed in Gonzales.

Will installation disrupt water in the whole house?

We shut water off for the connections that need it, then restore service and check fixtures. Most homeowners are back to normal use the same day on a standard whole-house install.

What is the difference between a water filtration system and a water softener?

Filtration is for taste, odor, and particles. A water softener is for mineral hardness that spots glass and leaves soap film. We install either, or both, when the home actually needs them.

Do you handle drinking water filtration only?

No. We install whole-house systems and kitchen reverse osmosis. If you only want drinking water filtration at one sink, we can keep the job that focused.
