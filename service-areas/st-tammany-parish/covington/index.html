Covington Installation Questions

What Northshore homeowners in Covington ask before an install.

Can you install reverse osmosis in a small kitchen?

Often yes. We check cabinet depth, drain access, and where the faucet can sit. Compact kitchens are a common Northshore install, not a deal-breaker.

Do I need whole house water filtration if I only care about drinking water?

Not necessarily. An RO system may be enough. Whole-house makes sense when bathrooms and laundry are part of the concern.

Do you also install in Mandeville?

Yes. Mandeville has its own service-area page, and both towns are on our St. Tammany Parish installation coverage.
