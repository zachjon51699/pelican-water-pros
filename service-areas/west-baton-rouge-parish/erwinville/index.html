Erwinville Installation FAQs

Questions from Erwinville homeowners and property owners before a filtration install.

Can you install treatment on a private well?

Yes. Well water treatment is a service we provide. The layout depends on the well setup, existing tanks, and what you want treated.

Is a whole house water filter enough on a well?

Sometimes, but wells often need additional treatment stages. We will not pretend a city-water carbon filter covers every well concern.

Do I need to be on city water to get reverse osmosis?

No. An RO system can be installed at the kitchen on well or city supply when drinking water is the goal.
