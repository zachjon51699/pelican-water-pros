Common questions

Before You Request a Consultation

Is the consultation free?

Yes. The form and the phone call are a free consultation. You get a recommendation for your home. You decide whether to install.

Which areas do you serve?

Baton Rouge and New Orleans are the two primary markets, along with Prairieville, Gonzales, Denham Springs, Covington, Mandeville, and nearby communities. Include your address on the form and we will confirm we can schedule the visit.

What is the difference between whole-home filtration and reverse osmosis?

Whole-home filtration treats water for the house. Reverse osmosis is usually installed at the kitchen for drinking and cooking water. Some homes use both.

Do you treat well water and city water?

Yes. City water and private wells need different equipment. Tell us which one you have, or we will sort it out from the address.
