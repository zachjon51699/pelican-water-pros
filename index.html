FREQUENTLY ASKED ・ ANSWERS TO YOUR QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions About Our Water Filtration Services

Get quick answers to common questions about whole home filtration, reverse osmosis systems, water softeners, carbon filtration, well water treatment, city water treatment, installation, and choosing the best system for your home.

What is the difference between whole home filtration and reverse osmosis?

Whole home filtration treats water throughout your house, while reverse osmosis provides advanced filtration for drinking and cooking water, typically at the kitchen sink.

Do I need a water softener system?

If you notice hard water buildup, spots on fixtures, dry skin, or reduced appliance performance, a water softener may help reduce mineral-related issues.

Can you treat both well water and city water?

Yes. Capital Water Co. offers customized treatment systems for both private well water and municipal city water across Baton Rouge, New Orleans, Covington, Mandeville, and surrounding communities.

What does a carbon filtration system help with?

Carbon filtration can help reduce chlorine taste, odors, and other common water concerns to improve the smell and taste of your home’s water.

How do I know which water treatment system is right for my home?

The best system depends on your water source, water quality concerns, household needs, and budget. Our team will review your needs and recommend the right solution.
