Privacy Policy

Pelican Water Pros respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information submitted through our website consultation forms.

Information We Collect

When you request a free consultation, we collect your name, address, phone number, email, selected service, and any message you provide so we can respond to your request.

How We Use Information

We use submitted information to schedule consultations, recommend water treatment options, and communicate about your request. If you consent to text messages, you may opt out at any time by texting STOP to our business number.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share details with service partners only as needed to complete an estimate or installation you requested.

Contact
