When Homeowners Need a Water Heater Replaced Quickly

  • Leaking water heaters: A failing tank can flood a closet or garage. If you can do so safely, shut off water to the heater and keep people away from standing water near electrical parts.
  • Complete loss of hot water: No hot water for showers, dishes, and laundry is disruptive. It may still be a failed part rather than a dead tank. Either way, the form and phone number reach our office.
  • Failed water heater tanks: Split seams, heavy rust, and tanks that will not hold water are replacement jobs, not temporary patches.
  • Urgent replacement needs: Tell us it is urgent on the form. We will discuss the soonest availability we can actually offer for that address.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer 24/7 emergency water heater replacement?

We do not claim 24/7, same-day, or guaranteed response times. If a tank is leaking or you have no hot water, call (225)-831-7583 and we will discuss the soonest availability we can offer.

What should I do if the water heater is leaking now?

If you can do so safely, shut off the water supply to the heater and the power or fuel at the dedicated shutoff. Stay away from standing water near electrical parts. Then call us or request a quote.
