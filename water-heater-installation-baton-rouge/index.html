INSTALL VERSUS REPLACE

Installing a New Water Heater Is Not the Same as Replacing One

Replacement assumes a heater is already there. Installation covers the homes that need a unit in a new location, a larger tank, or a first-time setup after a remodel. The plumbing, electrical, drain, and pan still have to be right. The difference is how much of that work already exists.

If the old tank is leaking in the same closet and you want another tank in that spot, start with replacement. If you are changing size, moving the heater, or adding hot water equipment where none is set up, this installation page is the better fit.

EQUIPMENT WE DISCUSS

Traditional Tanks, Electric Units, and Other Options

  • Traditional tank water heaters: Storage tanks remain the most straightforward install for many Baton Rouge houses. They need floor or stand space, a drain pan where required, and clear service access.
  • Electric water heaters: Electric tanks are the equipment we currently install and replace as a standard residential service. They need a dedicated circuit that matches the unit.
  • Gas water heaters: Gas tanks need supply piping and venting. We review gas jobs individually rather than listing them as a default install.
  • Tankless options: On-demand units can make sense for some upgrades, but they often need electrical or gas capacity that a tank did not. Tankless is discussed during a quote, not promised as a menu item.

SIZING AND REQUIREMENTS

Proper Sizing and Installation Requirements

  • Capacity: Household size and overlapping showers matter more than the gallon number on an old tank. We size for how the home actually uses hot water.
  • Garages, closets, and laundry rooms: Ground-level locations are common in Baton Rouge homes and are usually easier to reach than an attic.
  • Attic installations: Attic jobs often require extra labor because of access, removal of the existing unit if one is there, and getting the new tank into place.
  • Connections: Water lines, a temperature-and-pressure relief discharge, and electrical or fuel service all have to match the new equipment.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Water Heater Installation FAQs

What is the difference between water heater installation and replacement?

Replacement removes an existing unit and sets a new one in the same utility space. Installation covers first-time setups and upgrades where the home needs a new tank, different capacity, or a different location. Both still require correct sizing, connections, and a safe place to put the equipment.

Do you install tankless water heaters?

Tankless equipment has extra gas or electrical and venting needs. We can discuss those options during a quote, but tankless installation is not listed as a generally available service until it is approved for regular scheduling.

How do you size a new water heater?

We look at household size, fixture count, recovery needs, available space, and whether the home is already set up for electric service at the heater. Oversizing wastes space; undersizing leaves showers short.

Do new installations need permits?

Permit and code requirements depend on the property and the work being done. We review that during the quote instead of assuming every job is the same.
